Our Specialisations

What We Treat

Evidence-based treatments designed to deliver effective recovery.

SWD & Long Wave

Deep heat therapy using high-frequency electromagnetic currents to relieve pain, reduce muscle spasm, and improve blood flow.

Ultrasonic Therapy

Mechanical sound waves that penetrate deep into tissue to reduce inflammation, break down scar tissue, and accelerate healing.

I.F.T. & Tens

Advanced electrical nerve stimulation therapies designed for highly effective, targeted, and non-invasive pain management.

Laser Therapy

Low-level laser light applied to injured areas to decrease inflammation, reduce pain, and promote cellular repair.

Traction

Gentle decompression of the cervical or lumbar spine to relieve pressure on compressed nerves and treat herniated discs.

Muscle Stimulator

Controlled electrical impulses to elicit muscle contractions, helping to prevent atrophy and rebuild strength after injury.

Wax Bath

Soothing warm paraffin wax therapy that provides deep, penetrating heat for arthritis, stiff joints, and soft tissue injuries.

Weight Loss

Structured, scientifically-guided exercise and activity programs designed for safe, sustainable, and effective weight management.

Neck & Spine Pain

Relief for cervical stiffness, back pain, herniated discs, and posture-related strain.

Common symptoms

Morning stiffness, radiating pain, numbness, and difficulty bending or turning the head.

How our treatments help

Traction gently decompresses the spine to relieve nerve pressure, while SWD & Long Wave and I.F.T. & Tens effectively manage pain and reduce deep muscle spasms.

Joints & Arthritis

Targeted care for frozen shoulder, knee pain, osteoarthritis, and stiff joints.

Common symptoms

Pain during movement, swelling, restricted range of motion, and stiffness after resting.

How our treatments help

Wax Bath provides soothing heat for stiff joints, while Ultrasonic Therapy and Laser Therapy reduce deep inflammation and accelerate tissue healing.

Muscle Strain & Sports Injuries

Rehabilitation for ligament tears, muscle weakness, sports injuries, and post-operative recovery.

Common symptoms

Sudden pain, weakness in limbs, muscle atrophy, and inability to bear weight or perform athletic activities.

How our treatments help

Muscle Stimulators elicit contractions to rebuild strength and prevent atrophy, while Laser Therapy accelerates cellular repair for faster return to activity.

Weight Management

Structured, clinically-guided programs designed for safe and sustainable weight loss without injury.

Common challenges

Joint pain preventing exercise, plateauing weight, or needing a structured plan after surgery or illness.

How our treatments help

Our dedicated Weight Loss programs provide scientifically-guided exercise routines that protect your joints while effectively burning calories and improving overall fitness.
